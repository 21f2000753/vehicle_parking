**Vehicle Parking App - V1**
**ParkEasy** user-friendly app helps manage multiple parking areas and individual slots for four-wheelers. Designed for multiple users, it makes it easy to add new parking lots, track which spots are free or occupied, and keep a record of parked vehicles. The goal is to simplify parking management with clear and organized information. Whether used in residential buildings or commercial spaces, the app allows smooth coordination and hassle-free parking operations. It’s built to grow with your needs, with room for adding features like billing, login systems, and automatic slot allocation in the future.
